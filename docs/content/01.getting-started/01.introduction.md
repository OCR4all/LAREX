# Start here

LAREX helps teams turn document images into reviewed layout annotations and transcriptions, then reuse that work in exports, datasets, training, and evaluation.

If your organization already runs LAREX, sign in at the address it gave you. You can start working without installing anything.

## Follow a document through LAREX

1. **Organize.** Choose a workspace and create or open a project.
2. **Import.** Add images, XML, PDFs, an IIIF source, or a LAREX package.
3. **Annotate.** Draw regions and lines, or run an assigned Action to produce a starting point.
4. **Review.** Correct transcription, inspect validation findings, and complete assigned page Tasks.
5. **Reuse.** Export the project, create a release, or collect pages into a dataset for training and evaluation.

The [first-project tutorial](/getting-started/first-project) follows this sequence on one page. The [user guide](/user-guide/overview) gives direct routes to each workflow. Deployment and developer references are separate so you can consult them when you need them.

## Before asking for help

Check the selected workspace and the operation's status in **Jobs**. A missing button can reflect your role; a visible operation may still be blocked by a project lock, another editor, or storage quota. The [user troubleshooting guide](/user-guide/troubleshooting) explains how to distinguish these cases.

For a report to your administrator, include the affected project/page, the operation, the exact error message, and when it happened. Keep access tokens and share secrets out of reports.
